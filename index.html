01 / FEATURES

Three connected HR platforms

RossAI · recruitment and interview intelligence
  • AI Job Architect: Deterministic prompt design creates ready-to-publish HTML job descriptions from salary, technology, and reporting-line inputs; it also generates candidate outreach and LinkedIn posts. Perplexity Sonar Online can research a company to fill in missing culture, history, and benefits.
  • CV screening: Reads PDF and Word CVs with pypdf and python-docx, and uses Llama 3.2 Vision through Groq for image-based CVs. Extraction logs support troubleshooting. Candidate scores use Elite (90–100%), Strong (75–89%), Considerations, and Irrelevant groups; checks include ATS parsing success, generic AI-written fluff, career continuity (gaps), and currency (whether experience is current).
  • Interview operations: Weighted scoring produces an accept/reject recommendation and executive summary. Match reasoning explains scores, requirement saturation reports the share of job needs met, and skills are grouped as verified, gaps, or negotiable. The system drafts tailored interview questions from identified gaps.
  • Psychometric analysis: Flags unclear or shallow responses; assesses leadership style (laissez-faire, autocratic, or servant leader), communication style, and decision risk; recommends individual-contributor or leadership alignment; and creates profile-specific situational judgement tests.
Ross Dashboard · operations and candidate pipeline
  • Role-based workspaces: ProtectedRoute separates /admin/* administrator oversight from /team/* recruiter and manager workspaces. Custom Supabase authorization tables and PostgreSQL row-level security keep role data isolated.
  • AI-assisted intake: Recruiter CV uploads fill designation, age, total and relevant experience, current organization, reason for leaving, reporting manager, direct reports, current and expected compensation, notice period, and relocation permission. Client intake captures company, location, contact, roles, vacancies, and Active or Pending status.
  • Candidate status tracker: Moves candidates through Pending Review, Sourced, Internal Shortlist, Client Shortlist 1, and Offer Stage. Sourcing assigns a client; managers approve or reject with required comments, and rejection ends that candidate flow; client feedback records Approved or Rejected by Client; moving to Offer Stage reduces the client's open vacancy count.
  • Client pipeline: Pending-client feedback saves on blur. Promoting a signed prospect to Active migrates its information into the Active_Clients table.
Ross AI Assistant · context-aware company chat
  • A floating chat widget answers candidate and client questions using company term sheets and policies. Strict instructions keep answers inside that knowledge base rather than inventing policies or figures.
  • Conversation context is capped at the latest 20 messages. Candidate questions are routed to standard hiring channels without exposing management contacts; client questions about terms can be routed to business contacts.
  • The interface includes a collapsible history sidebar, typing indicators, aligned chat bubbles, and Markdown-rendered responses.

AI/ML HACKATHON FINALIST · CODE-A-THON 2.0

Beyond Horizon

A real-time space operating system and installable PWA that brings together 15+ live scientific data streams, satellite and sensor telemetry, interactive 3D visualizations, and generative AI for astronomy, Earth observation, and STEM learning.

React · TypeScriptThree.jsSupabaseNASA · NOAAGroq AIESP32
Explore the full technical breakdown

01 / PRODUCT & DESIGN

Mission Control for space and Earth data

Beyond Horizon combines 15+ live science feeds, edge-device readings, and generative AI in one real-time platform. It is designed for space enthusiasts and amateur astronomers tracking the ISS, solar weather, and sky events; STEM students and educators exploring orbital mechanics, climate, and planetary science; and citizen scientists following wildfires, air quality, and floods.

  • Freemium plans: Cadet provides the core dashboard, tracked events, and learning modules. Commander / Pro ($4.99 per month) adds unlimited AI image analysis, SMS alerts for solar flares and ISS passes, ad-free offline use, and high-resolution satellite layers. Admiral is the proposed enterprise classroom license for educator access to student XP, quiz scores, and progress through Supabase.
  • Future revenue: Telescope and space-tourism affiliate links, plus a planned Unified Space API that cleans and packages multi-agency aerospace data for developers.
  • Visual design: Midnight blue and void black with neon-blue and warning-red accents; glass panels over WebGL starfields and interactive globes; magnetic buttons, hover cards, hardware-accelerated 60fps Framer Motion transitions, and 8K mission-history assets.

02 / APPLICATION ARCHITECTURE

Web, graphics, and cloud services

  • Frontend: React 18, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4.1, Styled Components, Framer Motion 12, Radix UI primitives (accessible tabs, progress bars, and dialogs), and Lucide React. Local interface state uses useState; shared audio and session state use Zustand and React Context.
  • Installable PWA: vite-plugin-pwa with automatic updates caches **/*.{js,css,html,ico,png,svg} assets for read-only offline Commander access and standalone installation.
  • 3D engine: Three.js, React Three Fiber, and @react-three/drei. Canvas configuration manages pixel ratio, shadows, and sRGB encoding. Demand rendering saves mobile battery; InstancedMesh draws thousands of stars or asteroids in a single call; useTexture loads 4K/8K planetary textures lazily.
  • Visual modules: Drei Html projects React tooltips onto 3D bodies; Planetarium.tsx uses VirtualSky; SplashCursor.tsx renders fluid effects; Aurora.tsx creates procedural SVG motion effects with Framer Motion filters.
  • Backend: Supabase on PostgreSQL 15+ with Vercel Edge. The schema includes profiles (id, user_id, role, XP, rank), missions (id, name, agency, launch_date, status, image_url), progress (user_id, resource_id, score, status), and subscriptions (user_id, status, tier, current_period_end).
  • Access and real time: PostgreSQL row-level security uses JWT claims for Cadet, Commander, and Admiral roles. Supabase Realtime broadcasts global cosmic alerts on global:alerts and private achievements on user:{id} WebSocket channels. TypeScript/Deno Edge Functions handle /api/ai-chat and /api/process-subscription.

03 / SCIENCE & LIVE TELEMETRY

Orbital calculations and external data

  • Satellite orbits: satellite.js propagates SGP4/SDP4 TLE data in the browser to calculate current ISS position and velocity vectors. api.wheretheiss.at supplies position every three seconds; N2YO calculates overhead passes from user coordinates.
  • Astronomy: astronomy-engine calculates lunar phases, meteor-shower radiant visibility, and eclipses, including distance from the eclipse path of totality with Haversine geometry. SunCalc determines civil, nautical, and astronomical twilight and true-dark conditions when the Sun is 18 degrees below the horizon.
  • Planet positions: A custom zero-dependency Keplerian engine in nasaService.ts uses low-precision J2000 orbital elements for geocentric right ascension and declination, and detects conjunctions from angular separation.
  • NOAA space weather: GOES-R five-minute X-ray flux is grouped into A, B, C, M, and X solar flares. Planetary K-index data feeds geomagnetic-storm alerts and the Zhang-Paxton auroral oval; OVATION probabilities are mapped onto a 3D globe. Proton flux at or above 10 MeV is compared with NOAA S1-S5 radiation-storm thresholds.
  • Earth and climate: NASA EONET streams disaster coordinates for wildfires and floods; NASA GIBS serves near-real-time layers such as active fires and Black Marble night lights on Leaflet. The Global Warming API reports monthly temperature anomalies (for example, +1.40 C) against the 1951-1980 baseline.
  • Atmosphere: Open-Meteo and OpenAQ provide cloud cover, PM2.5, and NO2 measurements.
  • Launches and news: The Space Devs API (LL 2.2.0) supplies launch manifests with SpaceX API fallback. Spaceflight News API v4 combines global and ISRO news searches. NASA APOD falls back from API to RSS, then AllOrigins proxy scraping of apod.nasa.gov if rate-limited.

04 / EDGE SENSORS

Local environmental measurements

  • An ESP32 microcontroller runs customized C++ / FreeRTOS firmware and polls a DHT11 temperature-and-humidity sensor and an MQ135 air-quality, smoke, and pollution sensor.
  • The ESP32 posts JSON telemetry to n8n webhooks. n8n calibrates and validates the data before storing it in Supabase PostgreSQL.
  • A local Seeing Quality score weights cloud cover at 50%, humidity at 30%, and AQI at 20%. The system also estimates Bortle light-pollution class from AQI.

05 / AI SYSTEMS

Conversational and vision models

  • Astro Assistant / Commander Aurora: Groq-hosted llama-3.3-70b-versatile delivers low-latency inference (under 300 ms), with a strict Commander Aurora voice and a friendly Astro Guide voice. Multilingual Groq Whisper Large v3 handles speech-to-text; browser Web Speech API reads answers aloud.
  • AI navigation: The assistant can return a hidden [[NAVIGATE: Mission Name]] command so the client routes to a mission and highlights it with smooth scrolling.
  • Vision AI: Groq multimodal meta-llama/llama-4-scout-17b-16e-instruct (with Llama 3.2 Vision 11B/90B alternatives) analyzes uploaded astrophotography, identifies objects such as the Orion Nebula, extracts distance and scale, and awards 50 XP for a successful observation.

06 / PLATFORM MODULES

Dashboards, observatory, and learning

  • Dashboard: Brings together ISS paths, solar-weather alerts, launch countdowns, and XP. Browser geolocation with OpenStreetMap Nominatim reverse geocoding resolves coordinates into city, town, and suburb for local forecasts.
  • Earth Space Intelligence: D3.js and HTML Canvas project landmass GeoJSON as 60fps animated halftone dots over continents. NASA SMAP soil moisture and Sentinel-2 NDVI support regional flood-probability and crop-yield-stress scores.
  • Observatory: Las Cumbres Observatory VirtualSky uses stereographic projection to map right ascension and declination to a 2D sky. virtualsky.min.js and stuquery.min.js load only when the component enters view through Framer Motion's useInView hook, then unmount off-screen; live mode follows local time and GPS.
  • Discover: Combines general Spaceflight News searches with a parallel ISRO-priority search. "Did You Know?" polls howmanypeopleareinspacerightnow.com for the current orbital population and api.le-system.org for randomized planetary gravity data.
  • Missions: Regex maps payload names such as Falcon 9 and Starlink to local mission art. Historical Apollo and Voyager records merge with live launch feeds in one timeline.
  • Education hub: Cadet → Specialist → Officer → Commander → Admiral ranks. Quizzes award 10 XP per answer and modules or AI vision scans award 50 XP. Supabase upserts bind badges to JWT profiles; fuzzy matching tests image-based space-station and orbital-module recognition.

07 / DELIVERY & SCALE

Performance, operations, and growth

  • The report estimates a 10,000-user MVP at about $75 per month: $20 Vercel Edge, $25 Supabase Pro, and about $30 Groq usage.
  • For 1M+ users, the proposed plan adds Redis caching for NOAA and Groq responses and moves to Vercel Enterprise and dedicated Supabase AWS compute.
  • GitHub Actions runs lint and tests, creates Vercel previews, and manages Supabase SQL migrations. Sentry and Core Web Vitals track client errors and performance.
